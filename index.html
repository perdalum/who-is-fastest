How this is calculated

The comparison

For each driver the page uses their fastest lap with usable position and speed telemetry. Both laps are projected onto one shared centreline, split into 100 m sections, and compared section by section. A section is coloured only when the estimated difference is at least 0.03 s; otherwise it stays neutral — “too close to call”. Sections where either driver has no telemetry are hatched separately and excluded from the tally.

Lap and sector times are official timing. The spatial split is derived from ~4 Hz GPS speed and is indicative, not timing-grade. Same-driver calibration was used to set the 0.03 s band; it is a heuristic, not a guarantee.

What it does not do

It does not compare laps from identical fuel, tyre or traffic conditions. A later, lighter lap can look faster everywhere. It never invents data: gaps longer than 0.6 s make the affected sections no telemetry, which is distinct from “too close to call”. Monaco is excluded because its position stream has no complete lap.
